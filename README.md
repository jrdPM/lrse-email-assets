# LRSE email assets

Public image hosting for LRSE (Life Raft & Survival Equipment) email campaigns sent through Klaviyo.

- `offseason-service-2026/`: Off-Season Life Raft Servicing email (logo, workshop photo, social icons)

Images are referenced in email HTML by their raw.githubusercontent.com URLs, so keep this repo public and don't rename or move files once a campaign has sent.
